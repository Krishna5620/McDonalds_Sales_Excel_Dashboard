# McDonalds_Sales_Excel_Dashboard
Excel-based sales dashboard and data analysis project for McDonald’s business insights.

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing McDonald's sales data to identify business trends, customer behavior, product performance, and revenue insights. The analysis was performed using data analytics techniques to help understand sales patterns and improve decision-making.

## About McDonald’s
McDonald’s is the world’s leading quick-service restaurant 
brand, serving over 69 million customers daily in more than 100 
countries

## 🎯 Objective
. Analyze sales performance across different products/categories
. Identify top-selling and low-performing items
. Track revenue and order trends
. Generate business insights using data analysis
. Create dashboards and reports for visualization

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

. Microsoft Excel
. Pivot Tables
. Charts & Dashboards
. Data Cleaning Techniques

## Dataset Information
Questions:
• What is the total sales revenue for each category of menu items?
• How many orders are placed each day?
• Which menu item is the most frequently ordered?
• What is the total revenue generated by menu items?
• How does the revenue of each category compare over months?
• What is the average number of items per order?
• How do order volumes vary by time of day?
• How do sales trends differ across weekdays and weekends?
• How does the sales performance vary by category over different months?
• Compare the sales of top 5 menu items?

## 📈 Key Insights

1. Overall Performance
• Total Sales:$61,626.29
• Total Orders:5,370
• Average Items per Order:2.28
• Most Sold Item:Side Salad
👉This shows steady demand with customers ordering more than 2 items per bill on average.

• 2. Daily Order Trend
• Orders peaked in January (1,845).
• Dropped in February (1,685), possibly due to seasonal slowdown.
• Recovered in March (1,840), showing stable growth
